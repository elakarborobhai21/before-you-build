# Region pack: Latin America

Typically covers: Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru and the rest of Latin America, including the Caribbean where relevant. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. How common is cash-on-delivery for this product category, and what does it do to refusals, returns and cash collection?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (reviews, referrals, seller reputation on social platforms, a known brand, payment on delivery)?

## Region-specific checks

- How common are instalment payments in this category, and how do card instalments, local instalment schemes and buy-now-pay-later work in the launch country?
- Which instant-payment and local cash-voucher systems are used (bank-linked instant transfers, QR payments, cash vouchers paid at convenience stores), and what do they cost the seller?
- Which taxes (national, state, municipal) apply to this product, and how do invoicing and customs rules change the landed price?
- How does currency volatility or inflation change pricing, supplier contracts, investor expectations and the usefulness of a model built in local currency?
- How safe and predictable is last-mile delivery in the launch cities, and how do sellers handle theft, failed delivery and pickup points?
- How strong is WhatsApp and social commerce for this category, and how do customers expect to chat with a seller before buying?
- Which local marketplaces and regional players dominate, and how different are Brazil and Spanish-speaking countries in language, rules and platforms?
- How do regional venture investors and local funds view this category, and which holding structure do investors in this market expect?

## Local-language search terms

Search in Spanish and, for Brazil, Portuguese (with local variants in mind), and in English. Check local marketplaces and consumer-complaint sites, which show what customers really experience. Regulator notices and local financial press often precede English coverage.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> precio / preço em <city> (price, local word)`
- `<product> cuotas sin interés / parcelado sem juros (instalments)`
- `<regulator> <fintech / comércio eletrônico / comercio electrónico> normas últimas (latest rules)`
- `<product> opiniones / reclamações <marketplace> (reviews, complaints)`
- `<country> ronda de inversión startup este año (funding round this year)`
