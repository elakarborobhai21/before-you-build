# Region pack: Europe

Typically covers: the European Union member states, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and the wider continent including the Balkans and Ukraine. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. Is cash-on-delivery used at all here for this category? If not, say so in one line with a source; if it is, where and for what?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (independent reviews, trust marks and certifications, return guarantees, word of mouth, a known brand)?

## Region-specific checks

- Which GDPR obligations, cookie and e-privacy rules, and national data-protection regulator practices apply to this product's data use?
- How do VAT and the One Stop Shop scheme apply to cross-border sales of this product or service, and where does the company have to register?
- Which consumer-rights rules apply (withdrawal periods, returns, warranty, pricing display), and how do they change from country to country?
- Which product-specific EU or national rules apply (AI regulation, platform regulation, product safety and labelling, sector licences)?
- How different are language, payment preferences (bank transfer, direct debit, local schemes, buy-now-pay-later) and delivery expectations between the launch country and its neighbours?
- What public funding, grants, tax credits and national or regional programmes exist for this kind of company, and what are their conditions?
- What do employment rules, notice periods and works-council or union practice mean for hiring and for the cost of changing course?
- Which local champions or marketplaces dominate in each country, even where a pan-European brand exists?

## Local-language search terms

Search in English and in the launch country's national language, such as German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish or Dutch. National regulators, tax offices and chambers of commerce publish authoritative guidance in the national language first.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> preis / prix / prezzo / precio <country> (price, local word)`
- `<tax authority> OSS VAT registration <country>`
- `<regulator> <sector> rules latest (local language)`
- `<product> Erfahrungen / avis / opinioni (reviews, local word)`
- `<country> startup funding round this year (local language)`
