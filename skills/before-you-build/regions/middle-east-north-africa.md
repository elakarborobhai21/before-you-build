# Region pack: Middle East and North Africa

Typically covers: the Gulf states (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman), the Levant, Egypt, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia and Turkey. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. How common is cash-on-delivery for this product category, and what does it do to refusals, returns and cash collection?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (reviews, referrals, seller reputation on social platforms, a known brand, payment on delivery)?

## Region-specific checks

- Is Sharia-compliant finance relevant to this product or to its funding (financing, instalments, interest, investor terms), and what do local customers and investors expect?
- What is the difference between mainland and free-zone licensing for this business, including permitted activities, ownership, who the company may sell to, and visa and office rules?
- Which local sponsor, partner or ownership requirements apply to a foreign founder in the launch country, and how are they handled in practice?
- Is Arabic-first design (right-to-left layout, dialect, local voice and tone) needed, and how do customers react to translated-only products?
- Which government programmes, sovereign or state-linked funds and incubators back this sector, and what do they require in return (local presence, hiring, listing)?
- How do customers pay: cards, cash-on-delivery, local wallets, buy-now-pay-later, bank transfer? What differs between the Gulf and the wider region?
- How do religious calendar periods, summer travel and public-holiday patterns change demand and operations?
- How do expatriate and local-citizen customers differ in spend, language and channels, and which one is this idea for?

## Local-language search terms

Search in English and Arabic, and in French for the Maghreb and Turkish for Turkey. Arabic search often differs between dialects and written standard Arabic, so try both. Check government licensing portals and free-zone websites directly.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> سعر في <city> (price in city)`
- `ترخيص <activity> في <free zone / mainland> (licence for activity in zone)`
- `<product> افضل متجر / avis / en iyi (best shop, local word)`
- `<regulator> <fintech / e-commerce> rules latest (Arabic and English)`
- `تمويل شركات ناشئة <country> هذا العام (startup funding country this year)`
