# Region pack: East Asia

Typically covers: China, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau and Mongolia. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. Is cash-on-delivery used at all here for this category? If not, say so in one line with a source; if it is, where and for what?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (platform ratings, influencer and host recommendations, brand reputation, word of mouth)?

## Region-specific checks

- Which one or two platforms dominate this category (search, social, marketplace, messaging), and can a newcomer reach customers without paying them for the privilege?
- Which payment super-apps and QR wallets are used, and does a foreign company need a local entity or partner to accept them?
- What are the data localisation, cross-border data transfer, content and licensing rules for this product, and what approvals does a foreign-owned operator need?
- How much does sales rely on influencers, live-streaming hosts and group-buying, and what do those channels cost and demand from a seller?
- How high are customer expectations on delivery speed, packaging, after-sales service and product quality, and what happens to ratings when a seller misses them?
- How do convenience stores, pickup lockers and local retail chains work as logistics and payment points in the launch country?
- What does the demographic trend (age structure, urbanisation, household size) mean for this idea's addressable customer?
- Are there limits on foreign investment or foreign-currency movement in this sector, and which structure (partnership, local entity, offshore holding) do comparable foreign startups use?

## Local-language search terms

Search in English plus the launch country's language: Chinese (simplified or traditional depending on market), Japanese or Korean. Use the local platforms' own search, not only global search engines, since much product, price and review information sits inside apps and is not indexed elsewhere.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> 价格 / 価格 / 가격 (price) <city>`
- `<product> 推荐 / おすすめ / 추천 (recommended) <year>`
- `<regulator> <sector> 规定 / 規制 / 규정 (rules) latest`
- `<platform> seller commission and rules (local language)`
- `<product> 投资 / 資金調達 / 투자 (funding) <sector> this year`
