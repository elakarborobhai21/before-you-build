# Region pack: North America

Typically covers: the United States and Canada, with Mexico covered by the Latin America pack. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. Is cash-on-delivery used at all here for this category? If not, say so in one line with a source; if it is, where and for what?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (independent reviews, trust marks and certifications, return guarantees, word of mouth, a known brand)?

## Region-specific checks

- How do state-level sales tax rules, economic-nexus thresholds and provincial taxes apply to this product, and where must the company register to collect?
- Which state, provincial or federal licences and regulators apply to this business (money transmission, health, insurance, alcohol, education, lending)?
- How strong are privacy and consumer-protection rules in the launch jurisdiction (state privacy laws, federal laws, provincial rules, language requirements in Quebec)?
- How high are customer acquisition costs in the main channels (search, social, marketplaces), and who is already paying for those keywords?
- How do entity choice, residency and immigration rules affect a founder from outside the region setting up and raising money there?
- How much does the product depend on one platform (app store, marketplace, payments processor) whose rules can change?
- What do recent funding rounds show about stage, valuation expectations and investor appetite for this category, and how do Canadian and United States investors differ?
- What do employee costs (healthcare, benefits, contractor-versus-employee rules) add, and how does that change who the company can hire?

## Local-language search terms

Search in English, plus French for Quebec and Spanish for Spanish-speaking communities and for Mexico-adjacent markets. State and provincial government sites, court filings and trade associations are the key primary sources.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> pricing <state or province> (English and French)`
- `<state> sales tax economic nexus <product type>`
- `<regulator> <sector> licence requirements <state>`
- `<product> reviews reddit / forum <city>`
- `<sector> startup seed round this year <country>`
