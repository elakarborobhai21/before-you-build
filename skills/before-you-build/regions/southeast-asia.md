# Region pack: Southeast Asia

Typically covers: Indonesia, Vietnam, the Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Cambodia, Myanmar and Laos. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. How common is cash-on-delivery for this product category, and what does it do to refusals, returns and cash collection?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (reviews, referrals, seller reputation on social platforms, a known brand, payment on delivery)?

## Region-specific checks

- Which super-apps and regional platforms dominate this category (ride, food, payments, shopping), and does a new product have to live inside them, compete with them, or avoid them?
- Which e-wallets and bank-transfer or QR payment schemes are used, and are they interoperable across the launch country's borders?
- How important is live-stream and social commerce for this category, and how do sellers use it to build trust and close sales?
- How does the archipelago or multi-island geography change delivery coverage, lead times and cost outside the main cities?
- What are the rules on foreign ownership, local entity requirements and sector restrictions for this business in the launch country?
- How different are the rules, languages, payments and platforms between neighbouring countries, so that a second country is a separate launch and not an extension?
- How do marketplace sellers handle commissions, ad costs and platform fee changes, and how dependent are they on one marketplace?
- What are the main holiday and shopping-campaign periods, and how much of annual sales do platforms concentrate into them?

## Local-language search terms

Search in English and in the launch country's main language, for example Bahasa Indonesia, Vietnamese, Tagalog or Filipino, Thai or Malay. Many consumer-facing sources, marketplace seller forums and regulator notices are only in the local language.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> harga / giá / presyo / ราคา in <city> (price, local word)`
- `<product> review terbaik / tốt nhất (best, local word) <year>`
- `<regulator> peraturan <e-commerce / fintech> terbaru (latest rules)`
- `<marketplace> seller fee changes this year (local language forum)`
- `<city> startup pendanaan / gọi vốn (funding, local word) this year`
