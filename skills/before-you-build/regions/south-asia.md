# Region pack: South Asia

Typically covers: Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan and the Maldives. The founder's launch country is the primary market. Use neighbouring countries only as comparison or as expansion cases, and say so when you do.

This pack is a checklist of questions, not a set of facts. Answer each item with a source and an evidence label (`[S]`, `[E]` or `[A]`) as set out in `<PLUGIN_ROOT>/skills/before-you-build/references/conventions.md`. Check everything live. If an item does not apply to the launch country, say so in one line with a source and move on. If no source can be found, say so and label the answer `[A]` with the reason.

## Common checks

1. Which payment methods do people in the launch country actually use for this kind of purchase, and what fees does each charge the seller?
2. How common is cash-on-delivery for this product category, and what does it do to refusals, returns and cash collection?
3. Which logistics providers serve the launch cities and the regions beyond them, and what are their delivery cost, failure rate and return rate for this kind of parcel or service?
4. What does the festival calendar and seasonality look like for this category, including public holidays and shopping campaigns, and when do demand, delivery capacity and ad costs peak or drop?
5. What business registration is needed, how is tax applied to this revenue model, and which consumer-protection and data rules apply?
6. What is the funding climate for this kind of company in the last 12 to 24 months: which investors are active, which deals closed, and which have stopped?
7. Who are the dominant local and regional competitors, and what do they do that a newcomer must match?
8. How do people here decide whom to trust before buying this kind of product (reviews, referrals, seller reputation on social platforms, a known brand, payment on delivery)?

## Region-specific checks

- Which mobile wallets, instant bank-transfer and QR payment rails are used for this purchase, how interoperable are they, who regulates them, and what are the merchant fees and the cash-in and cash-out costs?
- Which regulator approvals or licences does a payments, lending or wallet-linked product need, and how long do recent applicants say approval took?
- What are real return and refusal patterns for cash-on-delivery orders in the launch city versus smaller towns, and what do sellers do to reduce them?
- How do festival seasons (religious and national) change demand, delivery lead times, staff availability and ad costs, and which weeks should a launch avoid?
- How does national indirect tax (GST or VAT) registration work for online sellers and for platforms, and what do marketplaces collect or require from sellers?
- Are there limits on foreign investment, foreign exchange remittance or paying overseas vendors and ad platforms, and how do local startups handle them?
- How reliable are addresses, and how do couriers actually locate customers (landmarks, phone calls, pickup points)? What does this do to delivery failure?
- How much of the target customer base is on a smartphone with mobile data, and how much transacts through social media pages or messaging apps rather than websites or apps?
- How do informal and family-run competitors (market traders, Facebook or WhatsApp sellers, wholesalers) price and sell in this category, and what do they offer that platforms do not?

## Local-language search terms

Search in English and in the main local language of the launch country, for example Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Tamil, Sinhala or Nepali, and also in romanised form (local words typed in Latin letters), which is how many people search and post. Check local news sites, regulator websites and seller groups on social media, which often hold more current information than English-language sources.

Example search patterns (replace the words in angle brackets with the idea's own terms, translated):

- `<product> price in <city> (in the local language and romanised)`
- `<product> দাম / कीमत / قیمت <city> (price, in local script)`
- `<regulator name> circular <payments / lending / e-commerce> latest`
- `<product> cash on delivery return problem seller group`
- `<city> startup funding round this year`
- `<product> best shop reviews <city> (local language)`
