Top 3 risks

Most serious first, from the Sceptical Investor's risk register and pre-mortem.

  1. Sellers will not pay what a paid rider costs. Moderate needs a blended fee of at least NGN 2,110 (USD 1.59) E (the fee at which contribution reaches zero), and NGN 2,290 (USD 1.72) E once VAT applies. The only sourced Island price is NGN 1,500 (USD 1.13) S, and informal WhatsApp riders charge roughly NGN 1,000 to 2,000 (USD 0.75 to 1.50) a run A. Likelihood and impact are both High. Early warning sign: more than half of the first 20 sellers A refuse the zone price.
  2. The cash runs out with no source of more. Fixed costs of about NGN 2.2 million (USD 1,653) a month E run ahead of contribution; founder cash is gone in month 7 E, and no raise or amount is named. Funding climate was not researched in this Quick Check. Likelihood and impact are both High. Early warning sign: cumulative spend above NGN 4,000,000 (USD 3,005) A before the decision.
  3. Cash loss or late remittance. A rider keeping one day's cash costs about NGN 360,000 (USD 270) E (20 cash parcels x NGN 18,000 A), which wipes out a month or more of Moderate contribution. No one on the team owns cash reconciliation (founder_fit.md), and sellers drop couriers that fail them (customer_demand.md). Likelihood Medium, impact High. Early warning sign: any unreconciled day, or any seller complaint about payout timing.

The test

The cheapest test in the next 90 days: a concierge pilot booked by WhatsApp and a form, with no app.

Version tested
the changed offer (zone pricing), not the original flat NGN 2,000 fee. The Sceptical Investor's recompute puts the original fee below zero contribution, so testing it would only confirm a loss. This is the same change as the pivot above.
What it tests
whether Lagos sellers will pay a blended fee near NGN 2,220 (USD 1.67) E (Island NGN 1,700 and Lekki–Ajah NGN 3,000 A at a 60/40 mix) while riders accept about NGN 1,590 (USD 1.19) E a drop (sceptic.md, question 9).
What you do
Founder 1 recruits 20 sellers A in Lekki, Victoria Island and Ajah and contracts 3 to 5 riders A at a per-drop rate. You run real paid parcels at the zone prices and log every cost per parcel. Before the pilot starts, get five courier quotes and five rider quotes, and pay for one legal check of the okada exemption, courier licence and cash remittance.
Budget
NGN 3,120,000 (USD 2,344) E (Founder 1 pay, a part-time dispatcher, tools and messaging, a recoverable cash float of NGN 500,000, Meta ads, seller sign-up credits, a parcel-loss allowance and the legal check; build-up in sceptic.md, question 10; each input A except the ad floor S).
Duration
2 weeks set-up plus 6 weeks live (56 days). Start 2026-10-20, live from 2026-11-03, end 2026-12-15. Black Friday and the December peak fall inside the window, so record cost per drop by week.
What you measure
contribution per booked parcel, C = (delivery fees collected + cash fees − rider pay for all trips − gateway and transfer fees − cash shortfalls − refunds and claims − discounts − messaging) / parcels booked, which is the money per transaction measure. Also revenue per booked parcel, R = (fees collected + cash fees) / parcels booked, and how many paying sellers are still booking in the last two live weeks.
Pass line
C − R x 7.5 / 107.5 ≥ 0 (positive after VAT), and at least 12 of the first 20 paying sellers A (60% retention, a working bar) still booking in the last two live weeks. Minimum sample: 300 booked parcels from at least 15 paying sellers A.
Fail line
C ≤ 0 over the minimum sample, or fewer than 10 of 20 sellers A (half, a working bar) accept the zone prices. The Sceptical Investor reads that as: the price sellers will pay does not cover a paid rider, so Stop. Between the lines, change the price once and decide.
Decide by
2026-12-22, before the decision deadline of 2027-01-31 in the brief.

Even a pass shows only that a parcel is positive after VAT on thin numbers: the Sceptical Investor's re-run of the zone-priced offer earns NGN 108 (USD 0.08) E a parcel before VAT and loses NGN 56 (USD 0.04) E after it at Moderate inputs, so the pilot has to beat that.

What would change this verdict

  • Money per transaction (Weak): measured contribution per parcel above zero after VAT over 300 parcels, from the pilot's own logs.
  • Customer pull (Weak): at least 12 of 20 pilot sellers A still booking after six weeks at paid prices, which shows they pay and stay.
  • Competitive response (Weak): evidence that district density and trusted cash remittance hold when Kwik or an informal rider cuts price. The pilot's retention in the last two weeks is the first read.
  • Rules and operations (Weak): written confirmation of the okada exemption, courier licence and cash remittance rules, and the VAT registration threshold, from the legal check and an accountant.
  • Team and commitment (Adequate): a named owner for cash reconciliation, and written clearance of both founders' employer terms.
