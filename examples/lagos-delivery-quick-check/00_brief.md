## The idea

- What it is: A same-day parcel delivery app for small online sellers on Lagos Island and the Lekki axis. Sellers book a pickup in the app by noon and the parcel is delivered the same day, with cash collected on delivery when the buyer pays that way.
- Who pays: The seller pays the delivery fee per parcel (some sellers pass it on to their buyer).
- How it makes money: A fee per delivered parcel, plus a small fee on cash collected and remitted to the seller.
